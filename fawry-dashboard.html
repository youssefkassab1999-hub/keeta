Branch practices

Customers say branch staff take their codes and sign them up for things they didn't ask for

Also reported: a third “contract” naming a stranger as first party, and staff pushing an “investment Visa” “because of the target”. The base is low, 8 posts, but the most-reacted post in the community is about exactly this.
