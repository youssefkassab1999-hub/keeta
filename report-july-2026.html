Trending Titles

1.2K conversations · released 2nd July

Underage turned discomfort into its biggest draw

The series sparked widespread discussion for tackling drugs, peer pressure, bullying, pregnancy scares, and teenage behavior — bold enough to divide opinion on whether its depiction of Egyptian high school life was realistic.

Positive 63% of conversations were positive; sustained buzz beyond the premiere, a finale praised for a well-paced conclusion (some comparisons to AlRawabi School for Girls), and one viewer calling it "binge-worthy — held my attention for four hours straight."

Negative The bold subject matter itself was the friction point — some questioned whether it represented local culture realistically.

294 conversations · released 26th July

Banat Omair won its audience without being the loudest title

89.4% of the conversation was positive — driven by relatable father-daughters family dynamics and praise for producer Hassan Assiri's fresh young cast.

Positive Engaging, fast-paced storytelling; widely shared praise for the young cast's performances.

Negative Some questioned cultural authenticity (absence of niqabs, girls socializing with boys in cafés) — but unlike Assiri's previous production Hay Al Jaradiyah, criticism never turned into direct backlash against him personally.

266 conversations · released 23rd July

A theme song did more work than the film itself

Bessan Ismail's performance of "Alom" — her first Khaliji-dialect song and first Netflix contribution — became the most talked-about part of the release, pushing the film onto regional trending lists shortly after launch.

Positive Strong pre-release anticipation; the soundtrack milestone celebrated widely. Negative reactions were minimal — just 1% of the overall conversation.

Negative A small number criticized Bessan Ismail's vocal performance as "toneless," unfavorably compared to singer Ahlam.
